The Pomodoro page now follows your colour theme. It was showing a scenic photo on every theme — including Purple, Forest and Rose, where the rest of Cuewise uses your theme's own colours — so picking a plain theme gave you a clean home page and then a full photo the moment you opened the timer. The photo belongs to the Glass theme, and the page header now matches the theme there too. Focus mode still shows the photo whichever theme you use, since that's the point of it.
