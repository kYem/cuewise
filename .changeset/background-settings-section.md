The background photo, your own image and the readability sliders now have a **Background** section of their own in Settings, instead of sitting under Focus mode. They were never controlled by the Focus mode switch — turning it off left them exactly as they were — and they set the wallpaper you see on the home page, not just in focus mode. Focus mode now holds only the things it actually turns on. Searching settings for "dim", "blur" or "wallpaper" takes you to Background.
